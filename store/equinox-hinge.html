Request Quote

The Equinox Hinge is a next-generation deployment mechanism for CubeSat and SmallSat appendages. Utilizing a specific arrangement of three composite tape springs (Buckling Composite Tape Springs), it leverages stored strain energy to effect deployment with high torque margins.

Key Advantages:

  • Self-Locking: The tape spring geometry naturally snaps into a rigid, locked state upon full deployment.
  • Shock-Free: Controlled release of strain energy minimizes deployment shock loads on sensitive payloads.
  • High Reliability: Monolithic composite construction eliminates friction-prone moving parts and lubrication needs.
  • Zero Backlash: The deployed hinge is structurally continuous, ensuring high stiffness and pointing accuracy.
