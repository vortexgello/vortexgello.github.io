Aurora Sail

Custom Order

Compact, deployable boom system for CubeSat solar sails. Solves common "blossoming" issues using a tri-boom configuration and solid carbon rods.

Request Quote View Specs

The Aurora Sail tackles the primary challenge of CubeSat solar sails: packaging a massive membrane structure into a compact volume without jamming. This system utilizes a 3-boom configuration with our proprietary guide system.

This "Tri-Boom" design increases available stowage volume by 30% and shifts tribology to a low-friction carbon-on-carbon interface, preventing deployment failures.

Advanced Development: We are actively engineering an optimized boom cross-section for industry-leading packaging efficiency. This evolution aims to maximize the stowed-to-deployed ratio, enabling significantly larger sail areas within the same volume.

Configuration Tri-Boom (3 Booms)
Boom Material Composite Slit-Tube
Guide Rails 3mm Solid Carbon Fiber Rods
Stowed Volume ~80mm Diameter (Tuna Can)
Sail Area 4 m² (Nominal 12U Class)
Sail Material CP1 Polyimide (2.5 μm)
System Mass < 800g (Target)
Actuation (Prototype) High Torque NEMA 17
Actuation (FM/QM) Geared BLDC Motor (Planned)
TRL 3 (Proof of Concept)

Connection Interfaces:

  • Standard: Solder Pads (Resistors) + Pico-Lock Connector (Data/Feedback).
  • High-Reliability: Harwin Gecko/Datamate (Integrated Power & Data) available on request.
