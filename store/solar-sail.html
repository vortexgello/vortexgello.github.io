Aurora Sail

Custom Order

Precision-engineered deployment mechanism for high-area CubeSat solar sails. Features a modular Tri-Boom architecture for industry-leading packaging efficiency and deployment reliability.

Request Quote View Specs

The Aurora Sail represents a significant advancement in small-satellite propulsion. Engineered to bridge the gap between heavy packaging and large surface area, this system utilizes a refined Tri-Boom configuration to maximize available stowed volume.

By integrating precision carbon fiber stabilizers, the Aurora Sail provides a low-friction, high-reliability solution for deploying large-scale membranes, ensuring mission success in complex orbital environments.

Configuration Tri-Boom (3 Booms)
Boom Material Composite Slit-Tube
Guide Rails 3mm Solid Carbon Fiber Rods
Stowed Volume ~80mm Diameter (Tuna Can)
Sail Area 0.425 m² (Scalable architecture; larger booms available on request)
Sail Material CP1 Polyimide (2.5 μm)
System Mass 140g (Excl. Motor) / 450g (Incl. Motor)
Actuation (Prototype) High Torque NEMA 17
Actuation (FM/QM) Geared BLDC Motor (Planned)
TRL 4 (Component/System Validation)

Connection Interfaces:

  • Standard: Solder Pads (Resistors) + Pico-Lock Connector (Data/Feedback).
  • High-Reliability: Harwin Gecko/Datamate (Integrated Power & Data) available on request.

Electrical Specs:

  • Actuation (Release): Burn Wire / Resistor.
    Recommended Drive: 5V @ 2A (current-limited) for < 2s.
  • Feedback (Status): Limit Switch.
    Dry contact closure upon full deployment (Normally Open).
