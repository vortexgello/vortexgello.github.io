Solar Sail Deployment System

Custom Order

Compact, deployable boom system for CubeSat solar sails. Solves common "blossoming" issues using a tri-boom configuration and solid carbon rods.

Request Quote View Specs

The primary challenge for CubeSat solar sails is packaging a massive membrane structure into a compact volume without jamming. This project utilizes a 3-boom configuration with solid Carbon Fiber rod guides.

This "Tri-Boom" design increases available stowage volume by 30% and shifts tribology to a low-friction carbon-on-carbon interface, preventing deployment failures.

Configuration Tri-Boom (3 Booms)
Boom Material Composite Slit-Tube
Guide Rails 3mm Solid Carbon Fiber Rods
Stowed Volume ~80mm Diameter (Tuna Can)
Sail Area 4 m² (Nominal 12U Class)
Sail Material CP1 Polyimide (2.5 μm)
System Mass < 800g (Target)
TRL 3 (Proof of Concept)
