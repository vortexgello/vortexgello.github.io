Scout Boom

Deployable Camera Mechanism

The Scout Boom is a deployable camera mechanism designed for capturing visual inspection data and "selfies" of the satellite in orbit.

It features an integrated flex PCB running along the boom length, eliminating complex wire harnesses and reducing snag risks. The system uses stored strain energy to deploy a bistable composite boom rolled compactly against the CubeSat.
